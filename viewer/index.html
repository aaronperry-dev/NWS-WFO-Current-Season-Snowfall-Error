How well the official WFO snowfall forecasts did in each snow event this winter: forecast − observed, season to date and event by event, on the native ~4 km grid of the observations.

Tick to show or hide an office. Click a row (or a CWA on the map) for details and to list only its events.

Events

Obs. / Fcst.: CWA-mean inches. Click a verified event to map its error; click it again, or press Esc, to return.

Snowfall log

Every stretch of measurable snow (at least 0.1 in on 1% of the CWA in a 6-h period), whether or not it is big enough to verify. Mean = CWA-mean total, Peak = highest cell, in inches. Verified events can be clicked.

How to read this

  • Red = the forecast was too high; blue = too low. Each cell is forecast − observed snowfall in inches. The map shows the season-to-date mean over all verified events (color range ±2 in), or one event when you pick it (±6 in).
  • Event: a stretch of snow with a CWA-mean total of at least 1.5 in (6-h periods of 0.08 in or more, gaps up to 12 h merged, split at 48 h). Snow that stays local also counts when at least 5% of the CWA gets 1 in or more; these are tagged local. Times are UTC.
  • Forecast: the official WFO-edited NDFD snowfall grid, the latest run issued at least 12 h before the event began. Observed: NOHRSC National Snowfall Analysis.
  • The public forecast archive runs about 5 days behind, so a finished event waits as “awaiting forecast archive” until it can be verified. Recent analyses can still be revised; they are re-read for 21 days.
  • A few events say little: season means from fewer than 5 events are flagged ⚠.
  • Hatched cells are excluded radar beam-blockage artifacts in the observation analysis.
